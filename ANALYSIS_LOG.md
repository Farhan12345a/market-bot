## Findings that have held up

- **Entry quality is the gap, not exits.** Stated 2026-08-19 at 23% WR against a
  43% breakeven; still true on 2026-08-26 at 36% against 39%. Every exit change
  since has moved the loss, not the sign.
- **Outcome is bimodal in MFE.** 2026-08-26: positions peaking above +1.0% went
  7/7 for +$528; positions peaking under +0.5% went 0/12 for -$594. Almost
  nothing in between. Watch the MFE table above for whether this repeats.
- **Take-profit tiers produce nearly all the profit.** 2026-08-26: 13 tier
  exits, 13 winners, +$514 of a +$539 gross.
- **Correlated bursts are one bet held N times.** 2026-08-19, 20 names in 9
  seconds, 1 winner in 23 for -$1,307.

## Findings later shown to be WRONG

Kept deliberately — each one looked reasonable on the data available.

- **"Widen the trailing stop to 1.35%."** Recommended 2026-08-24, cost ~$163 on
  2026-08-25. A trailing exit lands at roughly (MFE - trail), so a wider leash
  gives back MORE of the peak. Reverted; the breakeven floor solves the same
  problem without loosening anything.
- **"Drop earnings names whose EPS surprise is unknown."** Turned on 2026-08-25
  on ONE data point (PDD, -$86.56). On 2026-08-26 it dropped 15 of 15
  candidates — not a filter but a total veto, which would have made the whole
  earnings feature permanently inert. Reverted.
- **"Aug 26 was day one of continuation shadow data."** Wrong: the factors had
  been recorded but the journal's header was written once at file creation, so
  the 13 columns added since were invisible to any reader. The data was always
  there. Fixed by `src/analytics/csv_schema.py`.

## Open questions the data has NOT settled

- **Does `cf_score` predict anything?** On 2026-08-26 it was the second-worst of
  15 features at 15 minutes (rho -0.115, Q4 worse than Q1) and mildly positive at
  30 (+0.193). Two of its factors carry the wrong sign against their weights
  (`rel_strength` weight +0.20 vs rho -0.344; `vwap_pos` weight +0.15 vs rho
  -0.375). One session, 13 symbols — not enough to re-weight on.
- **Is `opening_hit_rate` real? Probably far weaker than it first looked.** It
  scored the strongest rho of any feature on 2026-08-26 (+0.332 at 15 min,
  +0.444 at 30, top quartile 100% hit rate) — but it is a per-SYMBOL constant
  fixed at screening time, and that day it took exactly TWO values across the
  whole session: 0.4 for CMG/IONQ/XPEV and 0.8 for the other ten. The rho is
  therefore a three-symbols-versus-ten comparison wearing 176 rows as a
  disguise, and the 0.4 group happens to contain IONQ (-$154), XPEV (-$35) and
  CMG (-$33). With a 5-day lookback the value is quantised to fifths, so this
  will keep looking strong on any day the low-hit-rate names lose. Needs many
  sessions and more granularity before it means anything.
- **Is the burst throttle discarding the better half?** Skips tagged
  `burst_throttle` returned +0.998% at 30 min vs +0.384% for trades taken
  (n=27, one session, correlated).
- **Does the 0.30% breakeven tier scratch winners?** Added 2026-08-26. All seven
  tiered winners that day traded below entry at some point without being
  scratched, because their dip came BEFORE the peak crossed +0.5%. A 0.30
  trigger reaches into that window. MFE/MAE do not record which came first.
- **Peak-anchored vs entry-anchored RESISTANCE.** Settled AGAINST entry-anchoring
  on 2026-08-26: the 10 positions where the two could differ made +$524.88 and 7
  tiered out, so it would only ever have altered winners.

## Known data-quality problems

- **`spread_pct` is unreliable.** 2026-08-26: COIN a constant 7.03-7.07%, ADBE a
  constant 5.078%, DASH a constant 4.561%. A 7% bid-ask on COIN at $184 is $13
  wide — stale or crossed quotes, not spreads. Any conclusion involving spread,
  including `cf_spread`, is invalid until `get_latest_quote` is examined.
- **Signals are not independent observations.** The same symbol re-fires every
  poll: 2026-08-26 had 176 signals across 13 symbols, IONQ alone 34 times. Read
  every rho against the SYMBOL count.
- **Per-symbol constants masquerade as per-signal features.** `opening_hit_rate`,
  `opening_avg_gain` and `opening_sessions` are fixed per symbol for the whole
  session, so their correlation with forward returns is really a comparison
  between a handful of symbol GROUPS, not 176 observations. A 5-day lookback
  also quantises hit rate to fifths — on 2026-08-26 it held just two distinct
  values. Treat any rho on these as n = number of distinct values, not n = rows.
